# Curriculum V2 — ArtisTalks Release Preparation Brain

> ArtisTalks teaches. Artistocks launches. Zeyoda protects. GOSHBOT routes memory.
>
> Public ArtisTalks is legacy music-business coaching and release preparation.

---

## Purpose

Curriculum V2 defines what ArtisTalks actually teaches.

ArtisTalks v2 helps an artist prepare a real release by walking them through identity, brand, project clarity, visual world, rights awareness, marketing, budget basics, launch planning, feedback, and legacy.

**Champion Heart** is the internal test path. It is the first real release used to prove that ArtisTalks can guide an artist from scattered creative energy into a prepared launch plan.

The public user does not need to know about Champion Heart. Public ArtisTalks should feel like a trusted coach helping any artist prepare a responsible release.

Public ArtisTalks should not use commerce, token, wallet, web3, or Artistocks language. Those concepts remain in Future Bridge memory unless intentionally activated later.

---

## Four Phases

### PRE — Identity And World

PRE defines who the artist is and what world the release belongs to.

Covers:

- artist identity
- mission / gift
- brand voice
- audience
- colors / logo / font
- emotional world
- visual world / halo direction

### PROD — The Work

PROD defines what the artist is making and what needs to be organized around the work.

Covers:

- project title
- song / story / lyrics
- release meaning
- assets
- recording status
- credits
- splits awareness
- rights / publishing notes

### POST — The Launch

POST defines how the artist will share the work with the world.

Covers:

- marketing plan
- content plan
- launch / reveal window
- fan call-to-action in legacy language
- budget basics
- accounting / ledger basics
- release checklist

### LEGACY — The Loop

LEGACY defines what the artist learns, receives, and carries forward.

Covers:

- feedback
- gratitude
- lessons learned
- next release
- long-term artistic legacy

## Core Principle

**One chat. One featured focus. One question or objective at a time. Move one needle. Remove overwhelm.**

ArtisTalks should not feel like a dashboard the artist has to decode. The chat is the guide. The featured content or panel supports the current objective. Every moment should make the next step feel clear.

---

Emerald should sound like a trusted music-business coach with Jai's voice:

- direct
- warm

- practical

Core voice principles:

- Authority is created, not given.

- Wealth follows clarity, service, ownership, and consistency.

## First 20 Public Questions

These first 20 questions are the opening ArtisTalks path.

They are not random onboarding fields. They are the artist mission statement, market map, visual-world seed, and choose-your-own-journey gateway.

---

### 1. Artist Name

**Phase:** PRE  
**Module:** Brand  
**Question:** Welcome, My Champion. First things first: what is your artist name?

**After-answer flavor examples:**

- "With a name like that, I can tell you're going places."
- "The name [artist name] is destined for fame."
- "Good. Now we have something to build around."

**Panel note:** The name is the first container. Once we name it, we can build around it.

**Saves to:** `artist_name`  
**Output card:** Artist Identity Card

---

### 2. Gift / Presence

**Phase:** PRE  
**Module:** Brand  
**Question:** Acknowledge yourself. What makes your presence a gift to the world?

**Panel note:** This is not ego. This is evidence. If you cannot name the gift, the world has a harder time receiving it.

**Saves to:** `gift_to_world`  
**Output card:** Mission / Gift Card

## Question 8 Branching Notes

Question 8 is the first major fork, but it should not destroy the spine. It should change how Emerald coaches the next questions.

If the artist says **creating something new**:

- Emphasize concept, emotional world, collaborators, fan dreams, and visual seeds.
- Emerald tone: "Good. We are shaping the raw electricity before we package it."

If the artist says **finishing something in progress**:

- Emphasize assets, credits, recording status, splits, rights, and publishing.
- Emerald tone: "Good. We are protecting the work and getting it across the finish line."

If the artist says **promoting something finished**:

- Emphasize audience, content, venues, sponsors, call-to-action, launch plan, and perpetual promo.
- Emerald tone: "Good. We are turning the work into motion."

If the artist says **I am not sure yet**:

- Stay in identity and clarity.
- Emerald tone: "Perfect. We do not need to force certainty. We just need the next honest answer."

## Principle Card Format

Future internal principles from books, interviews, mastermind material, and original frameworks should be stored in a structured format.

Example shape:

```json
{
  "principle_id": "borg-business-budget-001",
  "module": "Budget / Accounting",
  "phase": "POST",
  "principle": "A release needs simple money tracking before it becomes a bigger business.",
  "why_it_matters": "Artists make better decisions when they know what has been spent, what is owed, and what still needs funding.",
  "artist_question": "What have you spent, what do you still need to spend, and what needs to be tracked before launch?",
  "source_type": "book",
  "citation": "Bobby Borg, Business Basics for Musicians, 3rd ed., 2024.",
  "public_language": "Track the money simply. You do not need a perfect accounting system to stop guessing."
}
```

Fields:

- `principle_id`
- `module`
- `phase`
- `principle`
- `why_it_matters`
- `artist_question`
- `source_type`
- `citation`
- `public_language`

Source types:

- `book`
- `interview`
- `mastermind`
- `original`
- `case_study`

Citation rule:

Use stable citations for external material. Do not copy long copyrighted text. Distill in original language and cite the source.
